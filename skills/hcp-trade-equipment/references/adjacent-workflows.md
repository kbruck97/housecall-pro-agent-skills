# Adjacent workflows and authority boundaries

These retained lessons prevent the legacy equipment umbrella from expanding a narrow request into unrelated writes.

## Complete customer profiles

Separate private identity/address information, balance and status, exact job timeline, diagnoses/line items, job and customer attachments, property equipment and recurring themes. Validate customer scope of public and private lists independently. Invoice queries can ignore customer/job filters; match rows locally against exact identifiers. A capped list is not exhaustive history. Bill-to parent and contractor fields may exist only on a private surface; public tags alone may undercount relationships. Stale parent classification requires review, not guessing. Tagging or parent-type correction is a separate authorized workflow; read-only profile work must not perform it.

## Galleries and publication evidence

Use GET-only collection and preserve a method/status ledger. Separate technical scale, exact installed scope and photo publishability. A wide photo of several existing appliances does not prove the selected job installed them. Direct completed-job quantities outrank duplicated invoice groups for installed count. Property equipment and related service events are discovery leads, not same-job evidence.

Estimate identifiers can denote an option rather than its parent. Resolve parent and option with exact membership and record their statuses separately. An accepted option and completed job can coexist with a stale parent; never infer installation solely from proposal text.

Require exact photo-to-source-job provenance, permitted media use, private review of actual image content, and unresolved punch-list/safety disclosures. Expand to related same-property jobs only when authorized, label a phased project explicitly and retain each phase's provenance. Wrong-subject photos, brochures, representative stock and actual project media are distinct classes. No publish-ready winner is a valid result.

Customer feedback must be source-backed; no match means no satisfaction claim. Redact identity, addresses, access details, prices, faces/plates and sensitive filenames as appropriate. Public outputs must omit CRM IDs, private locators and signed URLs. Publication and website deployment need separate approval and verification; evidence review alone changes neither.

## Procurement and BOM readiness

Resolve the exact job before building a draft. Bare numbers may be display invoices, internal route identifiers or foreign-system items. A search returning 200 is not proof its filter worked. If exact identity cannot be resolved, ask for a canonical job link rather than drafting from a nearby result.

Audit service/material categories, estimate templates, current materials, approved-option lines, completed-job lines and invoices as distinct sources. A service ID does not select a deterministic BOM when reused across multiple nonempty material sets. Similar source-ID/quantity signatures are heuristic matches unless explicit provenance exists. Report unique, ambiguous and no-match cases.

Preserve material source identity, exact part number, purchasing unit and current cost. Separate labor, direct materials and installation allowances. A bundled install-material line is not a supplier-ready list; expand measured routes and accessories only when field values exist. Separate supplier versus subcontractor scope per validated job, never by universal trade assumption.

Default deliverable is `DRAFT / DO NOT RELEASE`, with unresolved vendor, supplier pricing, quantities, unit conversion, measurements, substitutions, code/site conditions and approval gates. Version new procurement archetypes instead of overwriting historical templates. Equipment evidence is not purchasing authority.

## Offline exports

Use deterministic strict projections into a separate sanitized artifact, with independent completion proof and only approved derivative media. Raw customer records, free-text descriptions, equipment names, filenames and unrestricted evidence URLs are not automatically safe structured data. Prefer invented fixtures for tests. Keep local runtime/private adapter data out of public example packages.
