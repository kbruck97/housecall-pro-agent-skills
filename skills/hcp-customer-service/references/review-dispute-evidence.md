# Disputed-review evidence investigation

Use read-only unless an exact external action is separately approved. Investigate allegations about a quote, diagnosis, payment, employee statement, service outcome or customer relationship. The output is an internal finding and redacted **draft/not sent** package, not a declaration that a negative review is fake.

## Collect and join

1. Preserve exact current review wording, rating, handle, source URL, timestamp and edit state privately. Notification email ellipses are truncation; require full text before attributing a disputed phrase or submitting an appeal.
2. Search recent jobs broadly before selecting a candidate. Look for at least three distinctive alignments such as timing, rounded amount, unusual equipment recommendation, service type, employee count and completion-to-review interval; check competing jobs. Handle/contact resemblance alone is insufficient.
3. Verify customer identity and complete available jobs, estimates, invoices, notes and attachments. Public versus private filters differ; validate row ownership and pagination. A list field called invoice_number may be a visible job number; cross-check actual invoice records. A blank invoice UI and an invoice-paid event are conflicting surfaces to report, not permission to discard one.
4. Join calls/texts through separately authorized provider adapters. Deduplicate multiple call legs by entry-point relationship and timestamps; row count is not call count. Search every relevant tenant sender/transport. Say “no events in reviewed ledgers,” not “no messages were sent,” unless transport coverage is proven complete.
5. Build one local-time chronology: contact, scheduling, job creation, arrival/start/finish, estimate/proposal, invoice/payment and publication. Calculate significant intervals with deterministic tools. Pre-service calls cannot establish what was later said on site.

## Grade claims separately

Use corroborated, contradicted, unsubstantiated or unresolved, with source pointers and missing evidence for each allegation. No retrieved written estimate proves only that no written quote was retrieved; it does not disprove a verbal quote. Rounded money language can remain substantially accurate. A real paid customer generally weakens fake-engagement/off-topic removal arguments. Do not describe the entire review as false when core facts are corroborated.

Consult the platform's current official removal policy before policy conclusions. Factual disagreement alone is weak removal fit; never invent spam, conflict, harassment or privacy violations. A narrow misleading-content appeal needs exact wording and decisive contrary evidence, not absence of paperwork. Weak removal fit calls for calm response/service recovery and better documentation.

## Draft and verify

Draft a neutral employee fact request: was the amount/range discussed; equipment/scope; actual amount; attendees; written/shown material. Do not contact employees or direct customer contact automatically. Prepare conditional appeal and privacy-safe public response separately.

Package: executive finding, match confidence/competitors, exact review-source limitation, local chronology, record comparisons, per-claim matrix, current-policy fit, prerequisites for appeal, unsent drafts, redacted exhibit index and no-contact/no-write statement. Remove private customer/contact/address data, source IDs, payment/signature data, private employee contacts, credentials and signed URLs. Keep raw originals private. Reopen the final document, scan extracted text, render every page and inspect clipping/redaction visually before delivery. A redaction overlay without removing underlying text is insufficient.

Synthetic positive: completed paid diagnostic visit matches three distinctive factors; invoice corroborates visit/payment, while an alleged oral replacement price remains unresolved. Negative: calling the reviewer fraudulent because the Estimates tab is empty. Publication and platform submission remain separate approvals.
