# Synthetic cases and editorial reconciliation

The cases below are invented and describe expected checks, not observed customers or executed publishing tests.

## Useful draft example

Synthetic source facts: a completed inspection record says a technician performed a pressure check; the attached image shows the instrument on the documented system; interpretation of the exact reading has not been verified.

Supported private draft: “A closer look at one inspection step: documenting the pressure check. The job notes and field photo help show what was checked.”

Unsupported rewrite: “Perfect pressure proves this home is safe and will cut bills by 30%.” Reject safety, perfection and savings claims; do not infer a testimonial or household identity. Even the supported draft remains blocked from release without rights, tenant branding, image inspection and exact approval.

## Lessons retained from source variants

The completed-job source contributed photo taxonomy, willingness to skip, notes-only stories, batch diversity, provenance, privacy, EXIF handling and actual render/collision checks. Its one-harvest photo percentages, company palette and trade-specific examples were not universalized. Its strongest “measurement proves quality” wording was narrowed to the actual evidentiary limits of a photograph.

Three marketing-engine main variants were compared rather than selecting the largest. The completed-job-relevant governance from the multi-client operator and multi-profile content references contributed tenant mappings, draft-only states, separation of creative and publisher authority, immutable target-bound approval, append-only consumption, canary stop and receipt/readback rules. Paid-ad setup, credentials, account bootstrap and unrelated tracking/competitive history were excluded.

The social-media main and local-service reference contributed canonical story provenance, rights/PII/authenticity, channel-native adaptation, release state distinctions and human community-risk routing. Historical channel endpoint/access claims were deliberately replaced with a current-policy discovery gate; no present platform support is asserted. The local-growth reference's review lesson—no sentiment gating or incentives—also informs the satisfaction package's separation of recovery from public-review selection.

## Offline documentation verification versus runtime proof

A structural check can verify frontmatter, package-relative links, synthetic schema consistency and absence of known private literals. It cannot prove HCP authentication, completion mappings, consent correctness, image readability, real API support, publishing idempotency or platform compliance. Report that distinction. Any later executable validator belongs in a separately authorized implementation with real test output and tenant-safe fixtures.
