# Completed-job content, proof before prose

Create private, evidence-backed local-service marketing drafts from completed Housecall Pro jobs and field media. A finished job supplies potential facts, not permission to publish. Default to **local drafts only**, with no social credentials, public media upload, customer contact, CRM write, or publication. The package is not a collector, renderer or publishing integration.

## Required context

Resolve tenant, exact completed-job scope, permitted source data, media ownership and use consent, approved brand/service-area facts, claim restrictions, intended audience/channel, draft output location and operator approver. If unavailable, produce a blocked intake/brief instead of a publishable claim. Unlike post-payment satisfaction outreach, content qualification does not universally require payment; any tenant billing/dispute policy is an additional gate, never proof of completion or rights.

Read [evidence selection and rendering](references/evidence-selection-and-rendering.md) and [package and release contract](references/package-and-release-contract.md). Use [synthetic checks and editorial decisions](references/synthetic-checks-and-decisions.md) before handoff.

## Discover contracts, do not invent tools

Identify actual available read-only job/notes/attachment/export capabilities, documented schema/permissions, tenant and property isolation, completion semantics, pagination, freshness and attachment download rules. A public HCP adapter, an optional internal surface, and an equipment intelligence mirror are different sources; do not assume parity or invent endpoints. If only an export is available, state its through-date and missing fields.

For media, verify authorized access, host allowlist, safe download behavior and local retention. Never forward HCP cookies/authorization headers to a third-party signed-media host. A signed URL is sensitive and not a public asset or a durable provenance key. For tools that can edit/render, verify supported formats, EXIF handling, crop behavior and output path. Publishing support must be discovered independently per channel and target; historical integration notes are not current platform capability claims.

## Production procedure (draft stage)

1. **Fix tenant and evidence window.** Separate job IDs from customer-thread IDs. Enumerate only the approved tenant/date range, with complete pagination or an explicit sample label. Record evidence snapshots and source times privately.
2. **Confirm actual completed work.** Reconcile job status, completion record, notes and media context. A proposal, estimate line, scheduled installation or nameplate alone does not prove the work occurred. Distinguish installed, repaired, inspected and serviced-existing scope; avoid turning a maintenance photo into an installation story.
3. **Classify source strength.** A candidate needs a usable photo OR genuinely specific, interesting completed-work notes. Classify media as hero/proof/documentation/unusable. Ask what the image supports, not merely whether it is attractive. Qualify measurements with context; one gauge image cannot prove an entire system meets a standard.
4. **Gate privacy, consent and authenticity.** Confirm rights for the exact purpose/channel/person/property and any employee voice/face. Redact incidental data and inspect the rendered derivative. Permission to document a job does not imply advertising permission. Unknown consent yields a blocked release; revocation supersedes a past approval.
5. **Build a canonical story.** Use a verified problem/finding/work/takeaway structure. Link every claim to a specific evidence reference and state limitations. Use broad approved geography, never an identifiable household. Do not fabricate quotes, savings, guarantees, safety outcomes, testimonials, urgency or availability.
6. **Select the batch.** Favor substantive jobs; skip routine work with no detail. Vary angles, openings and CTAs rather than restating one equipment type. Historical “about three per angle/text-card batch” is a review heuristic, not a target that justifies filler. Measure actual usable-photo yield rather than assuming historical percentages apply.
7. **Draft channel-native variants.** Keep one fact base with tailored copy, crop, caption/alt text, CTA and disclosures. Do not blindly cross-post or reuse a platform watermark. Use only actual media quantity; do not manufacture a carousel, slideshow or before/after.
8. **Render if requested and supported.** Apply approved tenant branding, EXIF orientation, subject-aware crop and readable typography. Inspect final pixels for PII, collisions, label occlusion and misleading crops. If no rendering capability exists, provide a labeled design brief, not a claimed image artifact.
9. **Validate and hand off.** Package provenance, rights, claim evidence/expiry, final media hashes, privacy review, exact draft payload and proposed target/schedule. Return accepted/skipped/blocked counts with reasons and the real validation state. Drafts must fail publication gates.

## Release boundary

A creative author cannot approve or publish its own package. Any later publisher must discover current official channel policy/access requirements, verify exact tenant-owned target, validate one immutable expiring single-use approval and execute through a separately commissioned adapter with preflight, find-existing, publish-once and exact readback semantics. These are contract concepts, not universal tool names.

Never interpret an approved draft/calendar as public authority. Uploading to a public media host is already an external disclosure. Never retry publication after a timeout or receipt-write failure until the prior outcome is reconciled. No network calls or publication are necessary to use this package for drafting.

## Stop conditions

Unknown tenant/job identity, incomplete completion proof, unsupported claim, ambiguous before/after pair, missing rights, identifying image content, revoked consent, brand conflict, stale facts or unavailable media blocks the affected candidate. Preserve the reason and offer a narrower supported draft only when it stays truthful and private. A text-only card can replace a missing photo; it cannot replace missing evidence.
