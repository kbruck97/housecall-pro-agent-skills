# Migration and conflict decisions

Do not copy installed sources over this repository or import their case corpus. Source variants are inventoried, not universally authoritative. The source-disposition ledger preserves distinct package variants even when mains match, and known notices remain in NOTICE.

## Semantic resolutions

- **Equipment mode:** installed-new creation requires completed installation and strict identity evidence; documented serviced-existing equipment may be a candidate with unknown model/serial. Neither mode grants mutation authority. Parent equipment, component and accessory counts are separate. Same model/different serial means separate physical units; duplicate photos do not.
- **Public versus private collection:** public GET jobs supports customer_id; historical alpha ignored-filter behavior is still a private-collection warning. Row-level scope and complete pagination always apply. Invoice filter is customer_uuid array. Failed private equipment reads cannot be replaced by an empty public result.
- **Current CSR versus legacy cadence:** default durable receipt/global consent/wrong-number/cross-lane rules supersede older “office will call” and fixed-timing examples. Tenant variants retain complaint empathy without technical/legal admission, trade-specific intake and SMS/voice differences; tenant brands/prices/addresses are external configuration only.
- **Happy calls:** source payment completion nominates a candidate, not permission to send. Dissatisfaction routes service recovery; public reviews must not be sentiment-gated or incentivized by imported historical examples. Referral/recognition programs require current approved terms.
- **Scanner:** source state, queue/cadence membership, actual provider delivery, lifecycle ownership and display state are different. A read-only scan never replays/enrolls/sends or fixes a board row. Dormant-maintenance audit is not happy-call outreach.
- **Official corrections:** read-only keys exist; rate reset is epoch; routes use per_page; estimate approval/decline is publicly documented but needs explicit option authority. Transition timestamp is not always approved-at. Split invoice aggregation uses invoiced_amount, not repeated full amount.
- **Attachment credentials:** replace authenticated shared-client downloads with fresh unauthenticated HTTPS clients for third-party signed attachments; redirects require fresh policy validation. Strip EXIF and customer details only in authorized derived marketing assets, not raw evidence.
- **Mutation discovery:** remove legacy create/delete probes, silent taxonomy/source-ID repair and blind retries. No hard deletion of old equipment to test a tool. Preflight → dry-run → scoped authority → submit once → exact readback; ambiguous outcomes reconcile before retry.
- **Source scripts:** private session collector and MCP bridge are deliberately not copied: they depended on host/profile helpers and actual server configuration outside their packages. Discover and implement adapters separately rather than shipping nonfunctional wrappers. Pure helpers here have no I/O to providers.
- **Adjacent source families:** infrastructure deployment, DNS, agent memory/accounting and archived gateways are excluded from HCP domain modules. Retain relevant enablement/rollback/outbound-canary lessons in webhook and communications gates, not host-specific runbooks. Disputed-review investigation uses a date-bounded neutral evidence timeline and separates allegation from corroborated fact; response/publication requires its own authority.

## Rights and provenance

The included RivetFlo-owned material is released under MIT with explicit owner authorization, including historically proprietary or undeclared material. Historical labels in NOTICE and the source-disposition ledger remain attribution, not present restrictions. Excluded source corpora, tenant data and third-party material are not relicensed.

This candidate is a privacy-safe distillation, not a lossless copy of thousands of historical case notes. Explicit source-family mappings do not imply every historical reference is redistributed or reviewed as current. Specialist and journey drafts read substantive main variants and selected reusable references; customer-bearing cases and backups were intentionally excluded. The equipment umbrella source main changed after discovery; its reference records this drift. Do not claim a frozen full-corpus byte-preservation audit.

Keep the private source manifest and variant-to-hash resolver under operator control outside this repository. No source-profile files were changed as part of packaging. Installed behavior, tenant onboarding and live capability testing are separate approved work.
